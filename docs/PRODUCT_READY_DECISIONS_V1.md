# MISCA — Product Ready Decisions V1

## DEC-2026-044 · Physical evidence freeze
No PDP material, fit, quality, durability or delivery claim becomes factual before physical evidence exists and is archived.

## DEC-2026-045 · Current quote before purchase authorization
Historical supplier references and prices are provenance only. Every sample/purchase requires a current written quote/specification.

## DEC-2026-046 · Golden sample is immutable and not sold
The approved physical reference for each product is retained as the production/QC benchmark and never enters sellable inventory.

## DEC-2026-047 · Product Ready is per product
Raíz and Ola can pass/fail independently. One ready product does not imply the collection is ready.

## DEC-2026-048 · Blank shortlist refresh
Historical T230/T265/X300 no longer determine sample priority. Current shortlist uses live 2026 evidence and direct reconfirmation before purchase.

## DEC-2026-049 · First sample prioritizes diagnosability
For Raíz and Ola, Direction A is sampled before the more complex challenger so failures can be attributed to blank/print/scale instead of panel-continuity complexity.
