## V32 blank decision

### DEC-V32-001 — Primary blank candidate: Tela pesada 265 GM

Use **Tela pesada 265 GM** as the first physical candidate for both launch products.

Reasons:

1. One reference can support both launch colors: **Raíz = Marfil** and **Ola = Azul Navy**.
2. 100% combed cotton with Pima hand / anti-pilling aligns better with the intended fashion/product positioning than the cheaper basics.
3. One common blank reduces size-chart, inventory, fit, care, production and supplier complexity.
4. It supports the techniques already present in the MISCA test dossiers: `RC-SCR-01 / RC-DTF-01` and `OLA-DTG-01 / OLA-DTF-01`.
5. Its blank cost is still low relative to the current retail hypotheses, leaving meaningful room for print, packaging, payment, logistics, returns, acquisition and taxes.

This is a **candidate**, not an approved master. Product Ready requires physical evidence.

The best approved sample is retained as the visual/quality master. The repeated winner becomes the wash-test piece. If neither Round A sample is acceptable, Round B is correction, not confirmation.

## Physical Product Ready protocol

Run each product independently. A pass for Raíz does not make Ola ready and vice versa.

### Gate A — blank and fit

Required evidence:
- exact reference and supplier;
- exact color code/name;
- actual garment weight/hand acceptable;
- S/M/L/XL measurements physically checked before publishing the final chart;
- neck, seams, hem and symmetry checked;
- before/after wash measurements recorded.

Proposed internal acceptance thresholds for first validation:
- measured garment should remain within ±2 cm of the approved size master before wash;
- preferred shrinkage after wash: ≤3%; hard fail if >5% in body width or length under the prescribed care test;
- no obvious neck distortion, seam twisting or color bleeding.

These are MISCA internal test thresholds, not claims from the supplier.

### Gate B — print

Required evidence:
- final technique selected;
- final artwork file archived;
- final placement and dimensions archived;
- no obvious edge failure, cracking, peeling, migration or unacceptable hand;
- repeatability between first accepted print and repeated winner;
- approved photo of front/back/detail.

Proposed internal acceptance:
- placement deviation between approved repeats: target ≤1 cm;
- no visible adhesion failure after wash 1 and wash 5;
- wash 10 should be observed before scaling materially, even if a controlled launch occurs after wash 5.

### Gate C — wash and care

Record at minimum:
- wash 0 measurements/photos;
- wash 1 measurements/photos;
- wash 5 measurements/photos;
- wash 10 measurements/photos;
- print condition;
- color condition;
- garment shrink/twist;
- final care instructions derived from the actual result.

Supplier catalog care guidance can inform the test but must not replace the MISCA validation.

### Gate D — golden sample

For each SKU retain one physical approved sample labelled with:
- product;
- blank reference/color/size;
- technique;
- artwork/version;
- placement dimensions;
- printer/workshop;
- production date;
- approval date.

No future production should silently drift from this master.

## Proposed commercial gates

These are MISCA management targets, not external facts.

1. Do not approve a retail price using blank + print alone.
2. Do not scale paid acquisition while net contribution before CAC is unknown.
3. Controlled launch target: positive contribution after fulfillment/payment/returns before CAC.
4. Paid validation target: establish a maximum CAC from real contribution, not from revenue.
5. Do not buy deep inventory before the first controlled sales confirm size mix, return behavior and conversion.
