## Same RFQ for every workshop
Quote **1 / 6 / 12 / 24 units** and separate:
- unit print cost;
- setup/screen/pretreatment;
- IVA status;
- artwork/prepress;
- sample charge and whether credited to production;
- sample and production lead time.

Confirm:
- customer-supplied 100% combed cotton 265 GM blanks accepted;
- Marfil and Navy compatibility;
- workshop liability for damaged blanks;
- maximum print area;
- placement tolerance and repeatability.

Quote placement bands: up to 20×30, 30×40, 40×50 cm, second location/front+back and lateral/side-to-back if possible.

### `RC-SCR-01`
Ask minimum quantity, colors, screen cost/color, underbase, ink type, curing, max area and lateral-placement tolerance.

### `RC-DTF-01` / `OLA-DTF-01`
Ask pricing basis, film/ink/adhesive, press settings, repress, max area, hand on 265 GM, Navy handling, wash recommendation and adhesion warranty.

### `OLA-DTG-01`
Ask dark Navy suitability, pretreatment, white underbase, front+back price, max area, color profile, curing and wash/color consistency.
